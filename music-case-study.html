🎧 Music On – Mobile Music App

Designing a seamless and intuitive music streaming experience focused on simplicity and usability.

🚨 Problem

Many music apps overwhelm users with complex navigation, making it difficult to quickly discover and play songs.

Users need a fast and simple way to access music without unnecessary steps.

👥 Target Users

Young users who want quick and easy access to music without complex navigation.

📊 User Pain Points

  • Too many steps to play songs
  • Confusing navigation
  • Difficult to discover new music

🎯 Goal

To design a clean, modern, and user-friendly music app that enables users to:

  • Quickly log in or sign up
  • Browse music effortlessly
  • Play songs with minimal interaction

👤 My Role

UI/UX Designer – Responsible for research, wireframing, UI design, and prototyping.

🛠 Tools Used

Figma (Wireframes + High-fidelity UI + Prototype)

🔄 Design Process

Research → Wireframing → UI Design → Prototyping → Iteration

🧠 Key Design Decisions

  • Used dark theme to reduce eye strain and highlight media content
  • Simplified login flow to reduce user friction
  • Maintained consistent spacing and typography
  • Highlighted primary actions using accent colors

🧩 Solution

  • Simplified navigation with clear hierarchy
  • Quick access to playlists and recent songs
  • Minimal design to reduce cognitive load

📱 Final UI Screens

🚀 Outcome

Designed a clean and intuitive interface that improves usability and reduces user effort in accessing music content. The app provides a smooth and engaging user experience.

📈 Impact

Reduced user effort by simplifying flows and improving accessibility of key features.

💡 What I Learned

This project helped me understand the importance of user flows, visual hierarchy, and designing for simplicity.

🔮 Future Improvements

  • Add personalized recommendations
  • Improve search functionality
  • Add micro-interactions and animations
